# Library

**Library** in the sidebar lists every game in the collection. Select a game
to open its [game page](game.md).

![The library as a grid of covers](https://ha-memory-card.pages.dev/images/library.webp)

_Game titles and cover art belong to their respective owners._

## Views

The buttons to the right of the sort pick how the library looks:

- **Cover grid:** a card per game with its cover, platforms and your play
  status.
- **Compact list:** a row per game, for scrolling through a long collection.
- **Copies table:** a row per copy, with its platform, region, format,
  condition, device, account and where it was bought.

The view is saved in that browser.

![The library as a table of copies](https://ha-memory-card.pages.dev/images/library-copies.webp)

_Game titles and cover art belong to their respective owners._

## Search, filters and sorting

**Search titles** finds a game by its title, its release titles and its other
names, ignoring case and accents, so `pokemon` finds `Pokémon`. Linking a game
to IGDB adds IGDB's other names, such as Japanese titles and acronyms. Add
your own under **Also known as** when editing a game.

The row of platforms under the search shows one platform at a time.
**Filters** opens the rest:

- **Show:** **Games I own**, **Wishlist** or **All games**.
- **Play status:** your own status for each game.
- **Platform**, **Device**, **Account**, **Bought at**, **Region** and
  **Format**.
- **Genre**, **Game mode** and **Label**. A label filter shows games with any
  of the labels you pick.
- **Players on the same screen, at least**, for a game night.
- **Hours to beat the main story, at most**, from IGDB's time to beat. Games
  without one are left out.
- **IGDB:** linked or not.

**Clear filters** resets them. Filters stay in the page's address, so a
filtered library can be bookmarked or shared with others at home.

The sort menu orders games by **Title**, **Recently added** or **Recently
played**. Recently added counts a game from when you first owned it, so a
wishlist game marked Got it moves to the top. Recently played follows your own
play.

## Buttons

Everyone sees **Scan barcode**; managers also see **Export CSV** and **Add
game**:

- **Add game:** type a title and select **Search IGDB** to fill in the details
  from a match, or save to enter them yourself. Set your play status if you
  already have one. Saving opens the game page, where you add its copies.
- **Export CSV:** downloads the copies that match the current filters. See
  [Import & export](import-export.md).
- **Scan barcode:** reads the barcode on a box. See [Barcodes](barcodes.md).

![Adding a game with matches from IGDB](https://ha-memory-card.pages.dev/images/add-game.webp)

_Game titles and cover art belong to their respective owners._

# Hardware

**Hardware** in the sidebar lists the consoles, controllers, accessories and
toys you own or want. Everyone can browse it;
managers add and change it.

![Hardware, with consoles, controllers and amiibo](https://ha-memory-card.pages.dev/images/hardware.webp)

Search by name, model, platform or notes, use the dropdown to switch between
what you own, the wishlist and all hardware, and pick a kind tab to narrow
the list. Selecting the copy count on a console, handheld or PC opens those
games in the [Library](library.md).

## Kinds

- **Console**, **Handheld** and **PC** hold games: each copy can say which
  one it lives on. **Plays** lists the platforms whose games it runs, so a
  PS5 plays PS5 and PS4.
- **Controller**, **Accessory** and **Toy or figure**, such as amiibo, hold
  no games. **For** names the platform they go with.

A device with copies on it cannot become a controller, accessory or toy.

Store accounts, such as a Nintendo Account or Steam, are not hardware. They
are under **Manage > Accounts**. See [Manage](manage.md#accounts).

## Add hardware

**Add hardware** asks for:

- **I own it** or **On my wishlist**;
- a name that tells it apart, such as "Living room Switch". Names are unique,
  so two of the same controller need names such as "Pro Controller (blue)";
- the kind and the model, such as "Switch OLED";
- the platforms it plays, or the platform it is for;
- region, condition, where it was bought and when, what it cost and what it
  would sell for now, serial number and barcode, as for copies;
- notes.

Inactive consoles are left out when choosing where a copy lives.
Deleting a device keeps its copies, with no device.

## Photos

The camera button on a card adds photos, such as the box or the serial
number label, up to 20 per item. Select one to see it full size. Memory Card
removes the location and other metadata a phone stores in a photo, as for
copies.

## Wishlist and stats

Switch to **Wishlist** at the top of the Hardware page to see hardware you
want. **Got it** opens the form as owned, to fill in where you bought it and
what you paid.

[Stats](stats.md) has a **Hardware** group with how many items you own by
kind, what they cost and what they are worth now, apart from the games.

## CSV

**Import & export** has a **Hardware** tab for a CSV file of your hardware.
See [Import & export](import-export.md#hardware).

# Cleanup

**Cleanup** in the sidebar brings together the tools that keep the collection
tidy. Managers see **Missing details**, **Duplicates** and, when IGDB is
connected, **IGDB**. Viewers see only duplicates.

## Missing details

**Missing details** goes through the games that lack details, one at a time.

![Missing details, ready to go through the games](https://ha-memory-card.pages.dev/images/missing-details.webp)

1. Under **Look for**, keep the details you want to fill in and turn off the
   rest. Each shows how many games lack it:
   - **Cover**, **Game modes** and **Player counts**.
   - **Copies:** games with no copies yet.
   - **Device**, **Bought at** and **Condition:** copies that lack them. A
     digital copy on an account has a place, so it does not lack a device.
   - **IGDB link:** games not linked to IGDB.
2. Select **Go through** to start.
3. Each game shows what it is missing. Fill in what you know, then **Save and
   next**. **Skip** moves on without saving, and **Stop** ends the round.

To upload a missing cover, select the cover. For games with no IGDB link, the
[close matches](igdb.md#close-matches) page is faster.

## Duplicates

**Duplicates** lists the games you own more than one copy of, with every copy
and what sets them apart.

![Games owned twice](https://ha-memory-card.pages.dev/images/duplicates.webp)

_Game titles and cover art belong to their respective owners._

The filters at the top narrow the list by how the copies differ:

- **Same release:** two copies of the same release, such as a spare.
- **Different platforms:** the game on two platforms.
- **Different regions:** two regional releases.
- **Different devices or accounts:** copies on two devices or accounts.
- **Physical and digital:** a boxed copy and a digital one.

A game can show under several filters. Copies on the wishlist are left out.
Only filters with games show.

## IGDB

Managers see this tab once IGDB is connected. It has **Sync with IGDB**,
**Review close matches** and **Review game titles**. See [IGDB](igdb.md).

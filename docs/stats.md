# Stats

**Stats** in the sidebar sums up the collection and your own play. The totals
at the top count the household's games, copies and wishlist.

![Stats in the dark version of the Default theme](https://ha-memory-card.pages.dev/images/stats.webp)

_Game titles belong to their respective owners._

## Your backlog and play

This group is yours alone:

- **Backlog progress:** how many of your games you have finished, and how many
  you are playing, have not started or left without a status.
- **Games by play status:** your games in each status.
- **Most played:** your games with the most sessions and hours.
- **Play activity:** a calendar of the days you played in the last 12 months.
- Your all-time play time and sessions, and this month's.

To compare everyone's play, open [Household](household.md).

## Collection

Copies by platform, by region and by store, the condition of physical
copies, physical and digital by platform, and **Copies added** over the last
12 months or by year. A copy counts on the day you bought it. Without a
purchase date it counts on the day it was added, or the day you marked it
Got it on the wishlist. A bundle counts once.

## Spending and value

- **Total paid** and **Estimated value**, with how many copies have no price
  or estimate yet.
- **Spent by year.**
- **Price and value:** what was paid next to what the copies would sell for
  now, for copies with both.
- **Most valuable:** the copies worth the most.

Prices keep their currency. Totals show one line per currency.

## Hardware

When the household keeps [hardware](hardware.md), a **Hardware** group shows
how many items you own by kind and how many are on the wishlist, what they
cost and what they would sell for now. The games' totals above leave hardware
out.
